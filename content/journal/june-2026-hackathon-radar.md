## How to read the fit column

"Fit" is a neutral relevance score for a builder working in **AI agents, security, and Solana** — 🎯🎯🎯 means it sits squarely in that lane, ➖ means it's off it. It's a lens, not a verdict; a $2M XPRIZE is still a $2M XPRIZE regardless of how many targets it has.

## What jumped out

- **The deadlines cluster.** Three of the strongest land on June 15 — worth planning around if you only have one build in you.
- **"Headline prize" ≠ "cash."** A couple of the biggest numbers are mostly software credits or whole-program totals; the corrections box untangles those.
- **Verify before you commit.** Four entries on the original list were already closed or didn't exist. A tier list is a starting point, not a source of truth.
