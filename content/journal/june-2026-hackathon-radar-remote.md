Two weeks ago I [ranked the 16 platforms](/journal/hackathon-platforms-ranked-2026) I'd actually trust to find paid hackathons. This is the follow-through: I opened every one of them and kept only the builds you can enter **without booking a flight**. One hard filter — remote-only — plus a one-week-minimum runway, stamped **as of June 16, 2026** (deadlines move; treat this as a snapshot, not a live feed).

By default the table shows the **enterable** ones. Flip "Show closed/dead" to see what I cut and why — including a few in-person events left in (greyed, 📍) so you can watch the remote filter do its job.

## How to read the fit column

"Fit" is a neutral relevance score for a builder working in **AI agents, Solana, and EVM** — 🎯🎯🎯 sits squarely in that lane, ➖ is off it. It's a lens, not a verdict; a $2M XPRIZE is still a $2M XPRIZE regardless of how many targets it carries.

## What jumped out

- **The whole board is AI agents.** Nearly every live remote hackathon this month is an agent build — memory agents, trading agents, on-chain agents, agent-commerce. If you ship agents, June 2026 is your month.
- **Headline ≠ cash.** The biggest numbers hide the most asterisks: one "150K" is a token, one "$2M" is a 90-day build-a-business track, and a couple fold GPU/credits into the figure. The prize-pool stat counts cash only.
- **Two platforms carry the field.** DoraHacks and Devpost host almost everything still open and remote. Colosseum and Superteam are between seasons; Devfolio, TAIKAI, HackerEarth and AKINDO returned nothing remote-and-enterable this pass.
